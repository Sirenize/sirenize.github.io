When I started high school, I drafted essay after essay trying to come up with the best story that represents my life in a fulfilling and earnest way. However recently, I have been enlightened on a new perspective on life that I believe writes itself. My life is not the story I decide to tell, the actions I make, or the words I say. Life itself has no alternate meanings besides just that. There is no one story in life. There is no definitive way to live and succeed. Life is about living and taking a stance on the world, and with so little time.

Since I was little, I've been terrified by death. It's this unfathomable fear of something you will never fully understand. I could live my life believing in whatever afterlife I can dream of,[a]but I know, in my heart, that it's futile. There will come a time in everyone’s life where they cease to exist. Their eyes will shut, their brain will power off. There will be nothing more to them than the memory that lives on in whatever history they made.

But I think that, in itself, is the beauty of it all. I’ve always seen people leaving their mark on the world and making it their mission to believe they meant something, but the truth is, nobody [b]means more than their mortality. So instead of picking the easy way out and believing in some scheme to make me appreciate myself more; I’ve learned to live. I’ve learned that above all else I am mortal, and I pave my own path.
