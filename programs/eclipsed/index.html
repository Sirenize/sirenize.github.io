Every night,

we talk about you.

Every night,

it’s all about you.

Every night,

I talk to the moon.

Because we’re not too different,

we’re alone in a sky filled with stars.

Our sun making us shine brightly through the night,

hiding when he shows his face.

You made me shine brightly,

you’re my sun.

But now I shine by talking to the moon.

Every night,

and it’s all about you.

Eclipsed

John Allard

I can’t believe you,

I can’t believe this,

I don’t want to believe it.

The world is gray,

the sun is black,

the trees have fallen,

there is no world to escape to.

Every moment of my existence has been filled with you,

I can’t live without you,

I won’t live without you.

There is no world without you,

I am nothing without you.

You can’t do this.

I don’t believe it,

I don’t believe it,

I don’t believe it.

Deny thy father,

forget thy name.

You have forgotten your oath

to my world.

I am no longer a capulet,

but a montague,

a rival to my father

for your love.

I did it for you,

have you forgotten?

“Call me but love, and I’ll be new baptized,

henceforth I will never be Romeo.”[a]

Traitor.

It’s been days.

I haven’t moved,

I haven’t slept,

I haven’t eaten.

I don’t deserve it.

I didn’t deserve you.

I can’t take this.

The world has fallen beneath me,

this  is my lowest point.

I want to take it back.

If I could go back in time

to when I was happy,

I would hold you,

be with you,

share a moment of silence with you,

one with no words but a thousand emotions,

knowing that it would all end,

I wouldn’t care.

Because I’d be with you,

even if the moment is just a memory,

it’s  you.

If I could go back in time

to when I was happy,

every second would be

with you.

You made me hate love.

Couples walking around,

knowing that they’re happy,

they found their “one.”

It’s daunting,

to know I’m behind.

Starting from the bottom,

regaining my footing with the world.

I know I’m gonna break again,

lose my footing,

fall to the bottom,

so why  try?

I found my “one,”

they just didn’t find me.

I don’t want to love you,

not as much as I do.

I wish you were a simple love,

one that runs away from me.

Escapes my mind and flies away.

But you’re stuck,

your wings are tied[b].

Unable to fly away and free my eyes.

I wish I could forget you,

I really do.

But the truth is, I won't.

I can’t break away,

free my wings,

run away,

From you.

I haven’t seen you in two weeks.

My parents are thrilled,

I’m sure yours are too.

We could’ve figured it out,

run away together,

dropped them,

taught them who we were.

Instead, you joined them,

proved them right,

taught me who you were.

I’m starting to forget you,

but not in the way I want to.

I can’t remember how it feels to be

held,

loved,

thought of.

That feeling I’d get when you cooked for me.

That feeling I’d get when we went out.

That feeling of being with you,

you’d reassure me,

“I’ll help you through it all.”

I took back my life,

yes,

you’re still lingering,

but I didn’t see you today.

I didn’t think of you all day,

because I don’t need to.

I took back my life,

and you’re not here.

I regained my footing,

climbed back up,

sure I might break again.

But I’m back,

and you’re not here.

There’s someone new,

and I don’t know how to feel about it.

I don’t want to move on,

forget the old path and start a new journey.

But I am,

and I don’t know how to feel about it.

It seems like forever ago,

when you ran into my dreams.

It seems like forever ago,

when you escaped just as quickly.

I don’t want to forget you,

I still want to love you.

But there’s someone new now,

and I think I’m starting to like this.
