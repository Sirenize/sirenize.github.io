Every friday night brings the same situation. As the football players clear the field for halftime, it’s time for the main event. We line up, waiting for the section leader to count us down.

        “3, 2, 1!” We run on to the field, screaming. Our performance has started. There’s something about those lights, that feeling that I get as I look into the crowd, seeing my family and friends who cam to see me– or enemies who have to watch me. That feeling gives me proof. This is what I worked so hard for. This is what gives me life.
