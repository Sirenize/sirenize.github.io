You’re my sacrifice.

I’d give my life for you if I could,

hell, I practically already did.

You make me wake up in the mornings,

you’re the reason I can’t sleep.

Sometimes it’s hard thinking about you,

knowing that you’re already lost.

But it’s harder to forget about you,

knowing that you’re everything I want.

Sometimes it’s a small price to pay,

but for you I'd give my life.

Sacrifice every waking hour dreaming about us.

There’s nothing I wouldn’t give to be with you,

because you’re my sacrifice,

and that will never change.

i can't see my world anymore.

you destroyed it,

piece by piece,

brick by brick,

tore it down to rubble.

like the strength of a trillion earthquakes

struck my city and turned it to dust.

you were my world,

but you destroyed me.

this  isn't easy.

everything is pushing against me,

forcing me to break through.

i use up my strength on the first hurdle,

just to be met with a crater to leap.

i'm  losing myself.

i used to

think,

create,

try,

i've lost that power,

creating worlds without lifting a finger.

i'm  losing myself.

i can't

think,

create,

try,

anymore.

something is different.

that glint in your eye,

the way you’d talk to me,

how you’d hold me,

it’s missing.

where are you going?

everything has meaning, right?

i'd like to think so.

but no two things share the same meaning,

nothing is ever the same.

like my blue hoodie.

yes, it's simple on the outside,

but to me it means hope.

life outside of mine.

i wore  it whenever I could,

sometimes multiple times a week.

trying to find its meaning.

until the day I broke,

life turned into a distant memory.

watching someone else control the vessel I called my own,

they hung it up.

gave up on the world,

turned pale and said goodbye.

but today I return,

taking it off the rack.

this is not a vessel.

this is not just a blue hoodie.

today, this is me.

tick, tick, tick.

please don’t break down.

tick, tick, tick.

not now,

not here.

tick, tick, tick.

i was  doing so well.

tick, tick.

whatever.

“oh how the mighty have fallen”

you’ve seen the world from both sides now,

one without power,

one without freedom.

the people you once knew,

destined to turn against you.

you knew from the start you would never win,

yet you’ve selfishly declined the people.

turning to the shadows,

blessing them with the sanctity of the unknown.

you made a world you don’t even trust,

we’ve seen your world from both sides now,

now you can’t even face seeing our power,

you can’t face our freedom.

i shouldnt have called you pretty,

it shouldnt have been you.

i wish i couldnt call you pretty,

i wish i couldnt think like this

i wouldnt have called you pretty,

if i didnt believe it.

i shouldve just said nothing

i shouldve just bit my tongue

i couldve just said "nevermind"

i couldve forgotten you

i wouldve said nothing

if i knew how youd react

im sorry.

ill wait for you,

across every world,

every universe,

every lifetime.

ill wait for you.

theres no time outside of you,

theres no air,

theres no freedom,

theres no life outside of you.

so across every world,

every universe,

every lifetime,

ill wait for you.

i had never noticed the leaves before

their slight hint of brown

the way they blow and fall in the wind

my shoe is untied,

and i have no one to tie it.

theres a little red thread tied around my finger.

for the longest time,

i couldnt tell you where it led to

so one day, i decided to pull it,

search for where it might take me

so i pulled and pulled and pulled

and it took me through,

the hospitals,

the cemeteries,

the schools,

the homes,

that i called a part of my life.

until i found the end,

theres a little red thread tied around your finger.

it connected us throughout our lives,

kept us together through it all.

theres a little red thread tied around my finger,

and its always led me to you.

my affairs are in order

the table is set

i’m running out of time

awaiting your return

i won’t be the last

i just want you to come home

please don’t be mad

i have no reason for anger

you have to understand me

if you do, i’ll listen

i’m  sorry.

i just can’t take it

i take it back

please just come home

i didn’t mean to do it

i wish i could take it all back

i’m sorry

please, it’s all we want

it’s so dark

is for you to come home

please?
