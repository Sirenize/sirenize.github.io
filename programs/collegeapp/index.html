When I started high school, I drafted essay after essay trying to come up with the best story that represents my life in a fulfilling and earnest way. However recently, I have been enlightened on a new perspective on life that I believe writes itself. My life is not the story I decide to tell, the actions I make, or the words I say. Life itself has no alternative meanings besides just that. There is no one story in life. There is no definitive way to live and succeed. Life is about living and taking a stance on the world, but with so little time.

        Since I was little, I’ve been terrified by death. It’s this unfathomable fear of something you will never fully understand. I could live my life believing in whatever afterlife I can dream of, but I know, in my heart, that it’s futile. Honestly, I envy those who genuinely believe in something along the lines of Heaven and Hell, because I know that (in the nicest way possible) I could never imagine something like that. Not to say I haven’t tried, of course. A long time ago I was struggling with this exact subject and decided to put my faith in God. I don’t think I need to tell you that it didn’t exactly pan out.

There will come a time in everyone’s life where they cease to exist. Their eyes will shut, their brain will power off. There will be nothing more to them than the memory that lives on in whatever history they made.

        But I think that, in itself, is the beauty of it all. I’ve always seen people leaving their mark on the world and making it their mission to believe they meant something, but the truth is, nobody means more than their mortality. So instead of picking the easy way out and believing in some scheme to make me appreciate myself more; I’ve learned to live. I’ve learned that it’s okay to not be okay. It’s okay to have these fears and doubts of my future. But above all else, I’ve learned that I am mortal, and I pave my own path.

Author's Note

I've never been able to truly capture this fear of death in any writing, so after a long night of struggling, I crafted what I believe to be a very beautiful and heartfelt essay/speech out of such a harsh fear. I've been doing lots of thinking into my future (which, in itself, could be it's own essay) and if I go to college and use this, or not go to college at all. It was still nice to have a representation of my fears. As much as I say that making a mark on the world means nothing, this is my mark.
