Show us “30 Seconds” of your Summer.

        As I’m speeding down Old State, I glance down at the clock above my broken radio. It’s 6:00. I’m late for practice. I swear, no matter how early I leave, I’m still always five minutes late. Mr. Dunn makes such a big deal about us being there on time, I’m praying I can sneak in without anyone noticing.

I finally make it to the school and run through the doors. 6:03. My heart is racing, I’m running out of breath. Running down the halls, the band room is in sight. Past the band doors, a group of students are gathered near the bus loop entrance.

        I begin to slow down, I send a confused look off in the distance after seeing the clock in the hallway read 6:05. I ask one of the guys in the group, “what’s going on?”

        “Dunn’s not here yet.”

What is something you have overheard? Did you want to hear it?

        I don’t know why this is, but I can’t stop listening to people. Ever since I was a kid, I could focus on conversations in the distance, my conversation, and the cacophony of different apps and games I have open through my over ear headphones. It’s come to a point where I can’t even sit in silence; If I can hear the AC buzzing through the walls of my house, I can’t work anymore.

        So to answer the question, I have overheard everything. I can’t choose not to. If I could stop listening, I would. I just think they’re way too interesting to care about fixing it.

A Photograph You Keep

        

        On a random Saturday morning, I wake up to my phone buzzing. Who could be calling me at noon on a Saturday? Way too early for my liking. Turns out, my cousins car broke down in a home depot parking lot and she needs me to come save her.

        I drive to the home depot and bring out my jumper cables of life, but of course, we’re dumb teenagers. We don’t know how to do anything. Random strangers try their best at helping, but the car’s battery is dead and needs to get towed. Oh well, I guess we get to go home now, and I can get some sleep.

        We have to wait for the tow truck. I  have to wait for the tow truck. I could be at home, sleeping in after a long game day. But instead, I’m sitting in a home depot parking lot, wasting my gas waiting for a tow truck to arrive (which did NOT come within the forty-five minutes it said it would).

        Around two hours later, I look up to see a man stepping out of his car. Immediately after seeing the tattoos and the hair I shout, “Is that Josh Dun!?!?”

        My cousin shoots up from the back seat, “there’s no way that’s actually him. Are you sure?”

        “Yes! I’m sure! I could spot him from a mile away.”

        “Well, go say something then… go ask for a picture.”

        Who does she think I am? A stalker? She jumps out of the car after seeing my hesitation, so I follow. We run inside after him and find him perusing the batteries.

        “Are you.. Josh Dun?” I ask.

        “Yeah. How are you?” My hero responds. To me. He responded to me. He talked to me.

        The conversation that follows is a blur, but I get the most legendary picture I will ever take and I will never. EVER. delete it.
