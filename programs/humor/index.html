why don’t people try? just, in general, why don’t people try? when presented with a challenge, you can either set your mind to it and complete it, or just give up and never think about it again. personally, i have to choose the first option. there’s something about looking around and seeing that i am in a room filled with opportunity that has been murdered by a lack of willpower.

recently, i’ve been giving myself challenges to prove that given time, anyone can accomplish anything. take a guess as to what i found… anyone CAN accomplish anything! obviously, you are not going to be better than someone who has been doing something for a decade, but you’ll be shocked to see how much one can accomplish with a week of dedication and commitment.

so why don’t people try? why can’t they just pick up a new instrument or learn how to code? i’ve always hear people say, “do 1% better than yesterday.” but why not 100? everyone is capable of doing whatever crazy thing they can imagine. all it takes is just 100% of your mind and effort.
