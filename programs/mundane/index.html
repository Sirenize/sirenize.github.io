According to a random statistic I found from google, 1 in 7 people around the world have a slight ringing in their ears. I am, unfortunately, one of those people. Throughout the years, I have never cared about how healthy or how good my ears are so I’ve always listened to my music at whatever volume I wanted, and in later years I wouldn’t wear ear protection when marching in drumline.

        So if nearly everybody has it, what makes me so special? Well, hold your horses you overachiever. Most people may see this as a defect. Something bad that happens to them that causes them to live their life in a worse way than before. At least I’d think so. But I see it as a symbol of my hard work. It only really started getting worse after I got really good at drumming, so there has to be some sort of connection… obviously. My hard work is measured not by any number, characteristic, or anything physical whatsoever. But in my ability to not be able to hear as much anymore.

Author's Note

This was a very last minute essay, I won't deny that. However I've been thinking about this very topic for quite a while. Definitely a fun challenge, writing about something mundane and spinning it into something that isn't.
