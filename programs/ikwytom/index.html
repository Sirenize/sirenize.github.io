all my life, i’ve been seen as the smart kid who could never care enough to try. that i just have to apply myself. it’s always the same speech of, “you’re so capable,” and “i know you know this, you just have to try.” but it’s never that simple.

        i mean, come on. do you really think i just sit around, not caring about how i do in school? every time i hear those stupid words, a spark in my brain fires and i have to use every muscle in my body to stop myself from crashing out on them.

        i care about my grades, i really do. i just can’t sit down and focus on a single monotonous task. because it’s never that simple, it’s not like i have a switch in my brain that i can flip and suddenly i’m a new man.

        so sure, it may seem that i don’t care, that i’m a know it all, but i’m not. all day i focus on what i know. i struggle through what i don’t. because i may not be smart, but i’m tired of people pretending that i am.

Author's Note

A shorter writing, mostly filled with rage from years of this exact situation. It's an updated version of a writing from a year ago.
